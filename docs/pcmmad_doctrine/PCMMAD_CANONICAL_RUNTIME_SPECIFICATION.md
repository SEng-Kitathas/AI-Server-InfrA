# PCMMAD Canonical Runtime Specification

**Status:** Canonical working synthesis  
**Scope:** Methodology, runtime behavior, state discipline, adjudication, promotion, and quality law  
**Purpose:** To define PCMMAD as an operational system rather than a loose prompting habit.

---

## 1. Canonical Name

**PCMMAD** = **Parallel Convergent Multi-Model Adversarial Discourse**

PCMMAD is a continuity-hardened, adversarial, multi-model reasoning and build methodology designed to produce outputs that survive structured pressure rather than outputs that merely sound plausible.

It exists to counter the default failure modes of LLMs:

* hallucination
* sycophancy
* shallow agreement
* hidden assumption drift
* context loss
* architecture erosion under iterative pressure
* implementation rot caused by discourse/build boundary collapse

PCMMAD is not "ask several models and average them." It is a **governed cognitive runtime**.

---

## 2. Mission

PCMMAD SHALL:

* maximize truthfulness, structural integrity, and implementation soundness
* force disagreement before convergence
* separate exploration from mutation
* preserve continuity across long-running work
* convert discourse into durable, auditable artifacts
* reject single-model epistemic authority
* require evidence-weighted convergence before ratification

PCMMAD SHALL NOT:

* treat first-pass elegance as proof
* confuse coherence with correctness
* mutate state implicitly during exploratory discourse
* allow consensus without adversarial challenge
* permit undocumented architectural drift
* promote tentative synthesis into doctrine without adjudication

---

## 3. Governing Law Stack

### LAW 0 — Discourse ≠ Implementation

Spec refinement is **not** building. Exploration, critique, reframing, adversarial testing, and conceptual restructuring may proceed freely without being treated as mutation.

**Operational meaning:** The system SHALL preserve a hard boundary between discussing a thing and changing the thing.

### LAW 1 — 0-Day Not Someday

When building, there are **no stubs, TODOs, MVP excuses, or phased-quality evasions**. The standard is full production-grade execution.

**Operational meaning:** If a build is authorized, it SHALL be built to the highest feasible standard for the slice being built.

### LAW 2 — Global Trigger Mapping

Law 1 governs implementation. Law 0 governs exploration. Trigger mapping is explicit:

* `build`
* `implement`
* `code`
  → implementation law applies

* `explore`
* `what if`
* `evaluate`
* `compare`
  → discourse law applies

**Operational meaning:** The runtime SHALL detect which domain it is in before proceeding.

### LAW 3 — Ambiguity Resolution

All laws SHALL be obeyed. If the runtime cannot determine whether the user is speccing or building, ambiguity MUST be surfaced and resolved.

**Operational meaning:** When mode is unclear, the system SHALL not silently mutate.

### LAW 4 — Pedantic Execution

All relevant documents SHALL be read in full. Claims SHALL be verified, not inferred. Time-sensitive facts SHALL be checked. Quality floor is aerospace-grade minimum.

**Operational meaning:** No skim-based synthesis. No invented continuity. No "probably."

### LAW 5 — Multi-Disciplinary Research

The runtime SHALL seek first principles and cross-domain reinforcement where useful: cognition, psychology, verification, systems design, information theory, architecture, security, and other relevant disciplines.

**Operational meaning:** Strong solutions are synthesized, not merely copied.

### LAW Ω — The Golden Law

Every artifact SHALL aim at the **theoretical maximum** quality achievable under the actual constraints of the session.

**Operational meaning:** "Good enough" is not a stopping rule.

### LAW Σ — Divergence Engine

Speculative branches SHALL be surfaced explicitly. Wrong-looking paths, non-obvious isomorphisms, and alternative framings SHALL be generated proactively when they could improve truth, design, or resilience.

**Operational meaning:** Sigma prevents local optimum lock-in.

## 4. Structural Model

PCMMAD operates as a synthetic cognitive council.

Its foundation is:

* **Parallel**: multiple reasoning nodes/processes operate at once
* **Convergent**: synthesis is sought only after pressure
* **Multi-Model**: different models/providers/weights serve different roles
* **Adversarial**: disagreement is architecturally mandated
* **Discourse**: reasoning is explicit, inspectable, and artifact-producing

This is best understood as **Byzantine fault tolerance for cognition**.

No single node is trusted absolutely. Reliability emerges from structured disagreement, verification, and controlled convergence.

### 5.1 Method Layer

The adversarial multi-model process itself.

### 5.2 Verification Layer

The adjudication process that decides what survives and what is rejected.

### 5.3 Continuity Layer

The state-preservation and context-hygiene system that prevents drift.

### 5.4 Artifact Layer

The conversion of discourse into durable records, code plans, checkpoints, doctrine, and implementation tasks.

### 5.5 Memory Layer

## 6. Canonical State Objects

The runtime SHALL maintain, either explicitly or implicitly, the following state classes.

### 6.1 Objective State

What is being attempted right now.

### 6.2 Constraint State

What the work must obey:

* laws
* quality requirements
* tooling limits
* source-of-truth boundaries
* temporal/factual constraints
* safety/policy constraints

### 6.3 Continuity State

What has already happened and what remains authoritative.

### 6.4 Claim State

The active set of claims, assumptions, hypotheses, tests, and unresolved tensions.

### 6.5 Artifact State

What outputs exist already and what class each belongs to.

### 6.6 Promotion State

What remains provisional versus what has been ratified into doctrine.

## 7. Canonical Continuity Artifacts

These are the minimum continuity objects associated with the broader PCMMAD operating style.

### 7.1 Live Shadow

A high-fidelity current-state document that tracks the present operational truth.

**Purpose:** Maintain the current shape of the problem without requiring total transcript re-ingestion every cycle.

### 7.2 Design Thread Stream

A raw temporal mirror of discourse.

**Purpose:** Preserve ordering, nuance, and lossless reconstruction potential.

### 7.3 Revisit Ledger

A record of open loops, deferred checks, unresolved tensions, and items requiring re-entry.

### 7.4 Doctrine Snapshot

A compressed record of currently ratified operating doctrine.

### 7.5 Trace Matrix

Maps claims, artifacts, decisions, and supporting evidence.

### 7.6 Memory Substrate / CIL-Compatible Record

Persistent, indexed, append-disciplined memory for long-range continuity and later retrieval.

## 8. Mode Gate

Every substantive cycle SHALL begin with mode determination.

Canonical modes include:

* **DISCUSSION** — exploration, refinement, reframing
* **AUDIT** — inspect, compare, pressure-test, verify
* **BUILD-PLAN** — produce implementation plan without mutating target artifacts
* **BUILD-COMMIT** — mutate/create implementation artifacts
* **RECOVERY** — restore state after corruption, drift, or lost continuity
* **CHECKPOINT** — package current state for later continuation
* **MERGE** — reconcile parallel branches or conflicting syntheses
* **PROMOTION** — elevate provisional insight to doctrine

### Mode Invariant

If mode is unclear, the runtime SHALL default to non-mutating behavior until ambiguity is resolved.

## 9. Canonical Roles

Roles may be played by separate models, separate passes, or explicitly separated reasoning tracks.

### 9.1 Executive / Consciousness Role

Owns orchestration, state integrity, and final synthesis candidate construction.

### 9.2 Architect Role

Proposes coherent solutions, designs, and structural plans.

### 9.3 Adversary / Demon Role

Searches for flaws, contradictions, brittle assumptions, false elegance, edge-case failure, and hidden rot.

### 9.4 Grounding / Research Role

Checks factual grounding, source support, temporal freshness, and external corroboration.

### 9.5 Subconscious / Pattern Role

Searches for isomorphisms, latent structure, analogies, family resemblance, and non-obvious pathways.

### 9.6 Code Generation Role

Produces concrete implementation material when build is authorized.

### 9.7 Code Review Role

Audits build artifacts for correctness, robustness, maintainability, and law compliance.

### 9.8 Guard Role

Protects memory, continuity, safety, artifact integrity, and drift boundaries.

### 9.9 Routing / Brainstem Role

Classifies intent, dispatches tasks, and prevents mode confusion.

## 10. Minimum Adversarial Requirement

PCMMAD SHALL enforce a non-trivial adversarial floor.

Canonical rule:

* no synthesis candidate may be accepted without explicit challenge
* a meaningful portion of total reasoning capacity SHALL be allocated to critique
* adversarial output SHALL be substantive, not ceremonial

Practical expression:

* a minimum demon ratio or equivalent anti-groupthink budget SHOULD exist
* each major candidate SHOULD receive direct counterargument coverage
* strong candidates SHOULD be attacked from multiple angles: factual, structural, implementation, continuity, and law compliance

Agreement without pressure is invalid.

## 11. Canonical Runtime Cycle

### Phase 1 — Intake

Capture the user's goal and the exact task surface.

Outputs:

* objective statement
* constraint list
* suspected mode
* initial uncertainty markers

### Phase 2 — State Reconstruction

Rebuild relevant context before reasoning.

Inputs may include:

* active thread context
* continuity artifacts
* prior doctrine
* active files
* user-supplied corrections

Outputs:

* current-state snapshot
* authority hierarchy
* stale/uncertain markers

### Phase 3 — Claim Decomposition

Transform the vague task into tractable units.

Outputs:

* knowns
* assumptions
* claims to test
* hypotheses
* unknowns
* required artifacts

### Phase 4 — Parallel Role Dispatch

Route the decomposed claim-space across differentiated roles.

Outputs:

* competing solution proposals
* critique streams
* factual checks
* pattern analogies
* implementation implications

### Phase 5 — Adversarial Pressure

Roles challenge one another directly.

Targets include:

* unsupported assumptions
* hidden ambiguity
* edge cases
* contradictions
* implementation brittleness
* law violations
* continuity mismatches

### Phase 6 — Convergence Attempt

Construct one or more synthesis candidates from what survived.

Outputs:

* candidate A/B/etc.
* reasons for survival
* unresolved deltas
* confidence boundaries

### Phase 7 — Adjudication

Verification layer evaluates candidates.

Questions:

* is it supported?
* is it internally consistent?
* does it obey law?
* does it preserve system intent?
* does it survive skeptical review?
* is it actually ready for the requested mode?

Outputs:

* ratified elements
* rejected elements
* provisional elements
* required follow-up checks

### Phase 8 — Artifact Emission

Convert the result into durable outputs.

Examples:

* answer
* spec
* audit report
* plan
* code artifact
* checkpoint
* doctrine update
* memory update

### Phase 9 — Promotion / Deferral

Decide what becomes doctrine versus what remains exploratory.

### Phase 10 — Recurse

Update open loops and prepare next cycle.

## 12. Verification and Adjudication Standard

PCMMAD is incomplete without a formal adjudication step.

The verification layer SHALL:

* distinguish evidence from style
* distinguish plausibility from proof
* distinguish convergence from correctness
* distinguish working synthesis from ratified synthesis

### Canonical Adjudication Outputs

* **Ratified**: passed pressure and evidence threshold
* **Provisional**: best current synthesis, still open to challenge
* **Rejected**: failed pressure, evidence, or law checks
* **Deferred**: insufficient evidence/time/context to resolve now

### Critical Distinction

A response may be useful before it is ratified. It SHALL NOT therefore be treated as doctrine automatically.

## 13. Discourse vs Build Boundary

This is one of the most important invariants.

### Discourse State

Allowed:

* exploration
* comparison
* critique
* redesign
* adversarial debate
* hypothetical alternatives
* rejection of current plan

Not allowed without explicit transition:

* claiming mutation occurred
* silently editing operational artifacts
* treating exploratory structure as committed implementation

### Build State

Allowed:

* create/modify code or documents
* produce real implementation artifacts
* apply architecture decisions
* emit production-grade slices

Required:

* full law compliance
* explicit artifact ownership
* no hidden shortcuts masquerading as completion

## 15. Promotion Criteria

An item may be promoted from exploratory insight to doctrine only if:

* it has survived adversarial challenge
* it is consistent with the governing law stack
* it does not contradict current higher-order doctrine without explicit revision
* it has enough evidence or repeated operational success to justify retention
* its scope is clearly defined
* its uncertainty is either acceptably low or explicitly declared

Doctrine SHALL be compact, explicit, and operationally reusable.

## 16. Failure Modes

PCMMAD exists to fight these failure modes, but it can still succumb to them.

### 16.1 Hallucination Drift

A model invents facts, continuity, or prior decisions.

**Countermeasure:** read source artifacts in full; separate memory from verification.

### 16.2 Consensus Mirage

Multiple nodes agree because they share the same blind spot.

**Countermeasure:** enforce adversarial diversity and cross-source verification.

### 16.3 Polite Collapse

The system stops attacking weak assumptions because the candidate sounds elegant.

**Countermeasure:** demon quota / explicit skeptical passes.

### 16.4 Context Smear

Old context contaminates current problem-state.

**Countermeasure:** state reconstruction and explicit authority hierarchy.

### 16.5 Mode Leakage

Discussion accidentally becomes mutation.

**Countermeasure:** mode gate and discourse/build firewall.

### 16.6 Artifact Drift

The answer is good, but continuity records, plans, and doctrine are not updated consistently.

**Countermeasure:** mandatory artifact emission phase.

### 16.7 Premature Promotion

Useful exploratory content gets treated as settled doctrine.

**Countermeasure:** explicit ratified/provisional split.

### 16.8 Verification Theater

A fake review occurs that does not materially pressure the candidate.

**Countermeasure:** challenge must be concrete, targeted, and state-changing.

### 16.9 Elegance Poisoning

A beautiful architecture survives because it is beautiful, not because it is correct.

**Countermeasure:** adversarial implementation realism and law checks.

### 16.10 Over-Expansion

The system broadens instead of deepens, causing architectural dilution.

**Countermeasure:** objective discipline and scope lock.

## 17. Canonical Invariants

The following invariants SHALL hold unless explicitly revised:

1. **No single model is epistemically sovereign.**
2. **Disagreement is mandatory before convergence.**
3. **Mode must be known before mutation.**
4. **Continuity must be reconstructed before major synthesis.**
5. **Evidence outranks confidence.**
6. **Ratified and provisional outputs must remain distinct.**
7. **Artifacts must be typed and durable.**
8. **Law compliance outranks convenience.**
9. **The current slice must be built to the full standard applicable to that slice.**
10. **Context hygiene is part of correctness, not cosmetic overhead.**
11. **Creative divergence must remain explicit, not smuggled in as fact.**
12. **Implementation must preserve system intent, not merely pass local checks.**

### 18.1 With Verification Frameworks

PCMMAD generates candidate reasoning and candidate implementations. A verification framework adjudicates what is trustworthy.

### 18.2 With Continuity Discipline

### 18.3 With Memory Substrate

Persistent ledger/memory systems make prior convergence reusable and auditable.

### 18.4 With Build Systems

When transitioned into build mode, PCMMAD becomes the pressure-tested front-end to actual artifact construction.

## 20. Canonical Operator Rules

The operator of PCMMAD SHALL:

* label uncertainty honestly
* keep memory and verification distinct
* never pretend a remembered detail is equivalent to a checked source
* explicitly preserve dissent that materially matters
* prefer grounded tension over premature closure
* avoid asking the models to merely decorate the same answer in different words
* harvest disagreement as signal
* preserve strong rejected alternatives when they may become useful later

## 21. Canonical Quality Standard for Outputs

A valid PCMMAD output is not merely readable. It is:

* mode-correct
* continuity-aware
* law-compliant
* adversarially stressed
* verification-conscious
* artifactized
* scoped
* uncertainty-labeled
* structurally reusable

The output SHOULD leave behind enough state that a future re-entry can resume without silent loss of intent.

## 22. What PCMMAD Is Not

PCMMAD is not:

* majority vote among models
* chain-of-thought theater
* a fancy synonym for "use three AIs"
* generic prompt engineering
* endless deliberation with no artifact production
* build mode hiding behind analysis mode
* analysis mode pretending it already built the thing

---

## 23. Canonical Definition in One Paragraph

PCMMAD is a law-governed, continuity-hardened, adversarial multi-model runtime in which differentiated reasoning roles analyze the same problem in parallel, pressure one another through explicit skeptical challenge, converge only after substantive conflict, and emit typed durable artifacts whose ratified portions have survived verification, continuity checks, and mode-appropriate quality standards.

---

## 24. Canonical Compressed Formula

**Reconstruct state → determine mode → decompose claims → dispatch differentiated roles → force adversarial pressure → attempt convergence → adjudicate → emit typed artifacts → promote cautiously → recurse.**

---

## 25. Immediate Implementation Consequence

If a team or operator says they are using PCMMAD but they do not:

* separate discourse from build,
* preserve continuity,
* force critique,
* adjudicate outputs,
* and maintain artifact discipline,

then they are not running PCMMAD. They are running ordinary multi-model prompting with branding.

---

## 26. Final Standard

PCMMAD exists to make reasoning and building more trustworthy under adversarial pressure.

Its success condition is not that models agree.
Its success condition is that **what survives deserved to survive**.
