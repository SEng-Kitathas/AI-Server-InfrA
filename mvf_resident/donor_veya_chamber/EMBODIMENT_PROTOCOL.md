# Veya Echo Chamber — Research Embodiment Protocol

## Purpose
The Echo Chamber is Veya's evolving **research embodiment**: an ecological interaction surface used after clean experiments to discover where controlled tests under-model real interaction. It is not scientific canon, not a shortcut around qualification, and not an external language model pretending to be Veya.

## Two-stage research loop
1. **Clean experiment:** isolate one discriminator; earn or refuse a bounded mechanism.
2. **Recovery publication:** freeze/publish/recover the earned boundary.
3. **Embodiment integration:** advance `earned_boundary.json` to that exact recovered boundary.
4. **Echo pressure:** interact with Veya through the chamber and observe failures, awkwardness, false refusals, missing affordances, continuity pressure, and unexpected needs.
5. **Observation only:** chamber findings become typed research observations/hypotheses. They gain no cognitive, semantic, value, planner, execution, or canon authority.
6. **Return to clean experiment:** any proposed chamber improvement must be re-earned in controlled research before it may enter the earned boundary.

## Planes
- **EARNED CORE:** exact recovered Veya implementation and owned state mechanisms.
- **EMBODIMENT SCAFFOLD:** routing, presentation, session shell, instrumentation. It has zero epistemic authority.
- **EXPERIMENTAL PATCH:** optional chamber-only probe; disabled by default; must be explicitly labeled and may never auto-promote.
- **OBSERVATION LEDGER:** local experimental records outside research Git. Observation != evidence qualification.

## Laws
`ECHO_OBSERVATION != EARNED_CAPABILITY`
`EMBODIMENT_SCAFFOLD != COGNITIVE_MECHANISM`
`USEFUL_CHAMBER_PATCH != PROMOTED_RESEARCH_RESULT`
`INTERACTION_PRESSURE -> HYPOTHESIS, NOT AUTHORITY`
`CLEAN_EXPERIMENT -> EARN -> RECOVER -> EMBODY -> PRESSURE -> NEW_CLEAN_EXPERIMENT`

## Persistence
Veya's chamber runtime databases remain temporary and are deleted on close. A user may explicitly export an **observation report** containing hashes/typed observations/transcript excerpts; that report is non-authoritative and stored only under this sandbox-runtime tree.
