# Veya Echo Chamber — evolving research embodiment

The Echo Chamber is the ecological embodiment paired with clean language/cognition experiments. It integrates only recovered earned boundaries, then exposes those mechanisms to real interaction pressure. Chamber observations are hypotheses, not authority.

See `EMBODIMENT_PROTOCOL.md` and `earned_boundary.json`.

Runtime state is temporary. Observation reports may be explicitly exported under `observations/`; they are non-authoritative and remain outside research Git.
