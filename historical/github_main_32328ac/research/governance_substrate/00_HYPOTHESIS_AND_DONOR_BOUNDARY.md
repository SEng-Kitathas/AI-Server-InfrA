# Governance Substrate Experiment

Status: LOCAL EXPERIMENT / DONOR RE-DERIVATION. NOT MICROSEED CANON. NOT GLOBAL PCMMAD DOCTRINE.

## Hypothesis
Mechanisms earned in ProtoAGI Microseed for authority neutrality, currentness, evidence admission, lawful composition, bounded autonomy, representation admission, and anti-manager pressure may be stripped from their organism context and re-derived as a governance kernel for PCMMAD architectural self-consistency.

## Donor boundary
Microseed remains a developmental cognition project. This experiment does not redefine it as governance architecture. Donor labels, ontology, organism structure, and canonical authority do not transfer. Only mechanisms/invariants/scars that survive PCMMAD-native hostile re-derivation may be admitted.

## Target problem
PCMMAD must keep authoritative state, derived state, agent-visible contracts, operator projection, mutation authority, recovery, migration, and global doctrine consistent without creating a new omniscient manager or allowing derived surfaces to manufacture authority.

## Candidate donor mechanisms
- emergent capability != emergent authority
- currentness as a first-class usability condition
- composition before new primitive
- research result != canon
- donor != authority
- bounded hierarchy != recursive/global manager
- submitted != started != completed != registered != promoted
- explicit admission/promotion gates
- representation may exist without semantic/truth authority
- hostile scaffolding-removal / assistance accounting

## Falsifier
Reject the governance-substrate hypothesis if it merely renames existing PCMMAD controls, creates a second authority universe, requires a central semantic/ontology manager, cannot detect real cross-plane contradictions, or adds more state than it compresses.
