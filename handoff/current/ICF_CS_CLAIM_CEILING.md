# ICF-CS v1.0 — Claim Ceiling and Nonclaims

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: BINDING ADDITIVE DOCTRINE WHEN QUALIFIED

ICF-CS standardizes continuity separation and rehydration control. It does **not** claim:
- that a current-ingress pointer proves the current state behind it;
- that timestamps establish authority;
- that historical handoffs or donor material are useless;
- that every project must use identical filenames, storage systems, or UI;
- that intent is factual truth;
- that all constraints are permanent;
- that frontier summaries are complete source contact;
- that green historical verification remains valid after consequence-bearing state changes;
- that a benchmark answer key is doctrine;
- that agreement with a benchmark key proves authority fidelity;
- that formatting and semantic correctness are the same property;
- that an expected label may be exposed to the evaluated prompt without changing what is being measured.

ICF-CS adds no product/domain/architecture/legal/regulatory/scientific-result authority. It governs continuity ingress, rehydration separation, currentness, provenance, conflict handling, and continuity-adjacent evaluation-data hygiene.

R4.4 remains sealed and unchanged. This addendum is independently visible until incorporated by a separately versioned successor or explicitly superseded additive doctrine.
